# Bosun Development

Before designing a solution or writing code, read the standards that apply to what you are changing, and follow them. The index is in [docs/developer/README.md](../../docs/developer/README.md).

Read the ADRs in `docs/adrs/` that touch what you are changing. They record decisions that constrain later work, and they carry the domain vocabulary. Use their terms rather than synonyms. If your design contradicts an ADR, say so rather than overriding it silently.
